Polished realistic 3D-rendered stadium entrance tunnel and reveal podium, dark navy/charcoal metal, warm gold trim, mint accent lights, volumetric rays, reflective floor, stadium bokeh, central dark negative space for a live card overlay. No people, baked cards, lettering, logos, EA/FIFA marks, controls or watermarks.

Generation used the built-in image tool. The project-bound copy is assets/pack_tunnel.png.
